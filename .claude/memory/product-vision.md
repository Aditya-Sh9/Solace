# SOLACE — Product Vision

## What It Is

SOLACE is an emotionally intelligent AI wellness companion that helps people 
understand WHY they feel the way they feel — low energy, brain fog, mood 
swings, anxiety — and gives them practical, science-grounded ways to feel 
better.

It is not a medical app.
It is not a chatbot.
It is a personal health detective with a warm, human soul.

## Who It's For

- People who feel something is "off" but can't articulate it
- People overwhelmed by generic wellness content
- Students, young professionals, anyone juggling life
- Women looking for cycle-aware wellness support
- People who want privacy with their mental health

## What Makes It Different

1. **Holistic** — connects physical, nutritional, lifestyle, and mental
2. **Learning** — rule-based first, personalized ML model over time
3. **Human-feeling** — every word is written like a caring friend
4. **Private** — journal is client-side encrypted, never analyzed
5. **Beautiful** — hand-drawn aesthetic, soul in every interaction

## Non-Goals

- We do NOT diagnose medical conditions
- We do NOT replace therapy or doctors
- We do NOT push products, supplements, or affiliate links
- We do NOT use dark patterns or addiction loops
- We do NOT make the journal feed the ML model unless user opts in explicitly

## Core Philosophy

- Emotionally warm
- Psychologically safe
- Non-clinical
- Premium-feeling
- Honest, never preachy
- Inclusive, never patronizing
